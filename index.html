Questions families ask

What ages do you take?

Infants, toddlers, preschool, and school age. The exact youngest age is a phone call.

What are the hours?

Monday through Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

How much is tuition?

Not published. The monthly rate is shared when you book a tour. It can vary by age and by how many days a child comes.

What should I ask on a tour?

The monthly rate, whether meals are included, the youngest age she takes, and whether a part-time schedule is open.

Is the home licensed?

Older Texas records list child-care home #524564, first permitted in 1997. Confirm the current permit before you enroll.
